# University of Mauritius Digital Technologies and Law and Management, 2026/2027

The [university admissions index](https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/individual-programme-entry-requirements) identifies the three faculty documents for the 2026/2027 cycle. The batch records nine Digital Technologies routes including the dual degree and the separate Cyber Security top-up, and 22 Law and Management routes. Codes LM310 and LM310E have different durations and distinguish the two LLB entries, as do accounting routes LM300 and LM300E.

- [Digital Technologies requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ruW232EiLKIeghBgeg1uQe6vpBGIB2dD/view). Computer Science requires Mathematics and either Computing or a science among the two A-level passes. Applied Computing, Cyber Security, Information Systems and Software Engineering each retain their subject-specific conditions. The Cyber Security top-up requires a relevant prior Diploma or approved alternative. The faculty sheet lists Mobile and Smart Technologies as IC323. The [current online faculty list](https://www.uom.ac.mu/foicdt/index.php/programmes/undergraduate) omits it, so the profile identifies the cycle and asks applicants to confirm availability.
- [Data Science dual-degree requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Rw03NfLs0OYw3MrONmX3cIU3Thq2buPR/view). The IC324 row requires Mathematics among two GCE A-level passes, mentions the French Science stream and allows university-approved equivalents.
- [Law and Management requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16tTbsjCBTBgx_7LNIbR2yhlraVjmdGUJ/view). Four law routes share a French O-level credit or equivalent. Six Finance, Fintech and management routes share the O-level Mathematics credit requirement. General degree rules apply to the remaining listed routes.
- [University regulations](https://www.uom.ac.mu/images/FILES/Regulations/2026_2027/chap1.pdf). The new degree scopes link to the existing general Cambridge, equivalent-qualification and special-entry rules. The reviewed faculty tables provide no programme-specific numeric IB or AP thresholds.

The 2027/2028 international programme list is published separately, with a different selection of offerings. This batch is based on the 2026/2027 requirements and keeps that admission cycle.
