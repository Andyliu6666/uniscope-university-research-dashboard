# The University of Queensland 2027 international undergraduate entry

Reviewed November 15, 2026. Physical PDF page 3 of the [2027 international entry score guide](https://study.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/2026-09/International-minimum-entry-score-guide.pdf) lists 54 programme rows. The numbers retain the guide's exact precision and scale. Scores include the converted IB Admissions Score (IBAS), UK GCE A-level aggregate, US AP aggregate, SAT, ACT and the corresponding ATAR. Twenty-three distinct published score sets are stored once and linked to the matching programme entries. Thirty-five of the 54 IBAS values include decimals.

The IB section records the original IBAS and identifies the official conversion. It carries separate coverage from raw published IB Diploma totals in the database. The source row retains intake and quota scope. Seventeen rows are marked by the guide as having elevated English requirements. [UQ IB and ATAR explanation](https://study.uq.edu.au/stories/ib-vs-atar), [UQ US high school qualifications](https://study.uq.edu.au/sites/default/files/2020-06/usa-high-school-info-sheet.pdf).

## Programme-specific additions

- Doctor of Medicine provisional entry for school leavers shows IBAS 38.75, UK GCE 14, AP 16, SAT 1380 and ACT 32 in the guide. The separate admissions page requires UCAT ANZ and an MMI. It describes bachelor-degree progression and the international direct-application deadline. [MD provisional entry requirements](https://study.uq.edu.au/admissions/doctor-medicine/pathways-high-school-leavers/provisional-entry/entry-requirements), [application and dates](https://study.uq.edu.au/admissions/doctor-medicine/pathways-high-school-leavers/provisional-entry/how-to-apply).
- Veterinary Science records Casper and the academic subjects. The international table gives rank 94 and IBAS 38. The 2027 programme page also gives rank 95 in its selection section. The page and table figures are shown with their contexts. [Veterinary Science 2027](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-veterinary-science-honours-2378?year=2027).
- Dental Science records its UCAT and Chemistry prerequisites. Music records the audition and interview and the musicianship criterion listed in the guide. Computer Science records English and Mathematical Methods. Nutrition and Dietetics records the required subject groups and its higher English requirement. [Dental Science](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-dental-science-honours-2367?studentType=international), [Music](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-music-2535?year=2027), [Computer Science](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-computer-science-2559?year=2027), [Nutrition and Dietetics](https://study.uq.edu.au/study-options/programs/bachelor-nutrition-sciences-master-dietetics-studies-2534?year=2027).
