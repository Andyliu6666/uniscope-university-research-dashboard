Some alternative qualifications involve an interview.
- [Biomedical Sciences](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/biosciences/bsc-biomedical-sciences/) uses AAA or A*AB, typical IB 36 with flexible Higher Level grade combinations and Bio/Chem subject grades, and three AP exams at 5 including Biology and Chemistry. Its contextual IB offer also totals 36 but uses different Higher Level grades. Admissions considers statements in Biomedical Sciences, medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine or related health disciplines.
