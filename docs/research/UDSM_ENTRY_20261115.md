# University of Dar es Salaam undergraduate direct entry, 2026/2027

The batch matches all 107 UDSM direct secondary-entry programme codes in the Tanzania Commission for Universities (TCU) guide, printed pages 273–307. The programme rows record academic subjects, grades, total ACSEE points, O-level requirements where listed, programme length and cycle-specific published intake capacity. Each programme fact links to its printed page and exact code. Shared subject and institutional requirements are stored once and attached to each applicable programme.

The international scope covers foreign secondary qualifications, recognition and equivalence documents, the application route and English evidence where applicable.

Programme identity uses the TCU code. This resolves repeated or ambiguous university headings, including Mass Communication and Multimedia (UD038), Public Relations (UD039) and Mathematics (UD089). The degree names for UD001, UD006 and UD015 follow the corresponding TCU rows, and the spelling for UD105 has been normalised to Geology. UD108 Industrial Biotechnology includes a separate note on the TCU mathematics and business route and the UDSM science and O-level requirements pending university confirmation. Medicine (UD080) and Dental Surgery (UD098) each have a six-point ACSEE threshold and require a D in each of Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

ACSEE is the Tanzanian advanced secondary qualification. The reviewed sources do not specify universal programme scores for the IB Diploma, Cambridge International A Level or AP. The intake figures describe capacity for this cycle. The verified profiles are scoped to first-year undergraduate direct entry. Diploma routes and other admission years need their own source review.
