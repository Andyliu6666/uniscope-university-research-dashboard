# Kenya university-direct programme requirements

- [Nairobi's Dental Sciences department](https://dental.uonbi.ac.ke/programs-content-type/bachelor-dental-surgery) lists IB grade 5 in Biology, Chemistry and Physics or Mathematics for V28. It lists three A-level principal passes at C or higher in those subjects. The [Health Sciences overview](https://healthsciences.uonbi.ac.ke/node/26) describes a separate principal-plus-subsidiary route, so both official references are visible. The score refers to an IB subject grade and the source gives no overall diploma score.
- [Nairobi Microbiology and Biotechnology](https://biology.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-science-microbiology-and-biotechnology) lists a minimum IB grade of 5 in Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics. It also lists two KACE or GCE principal passes in Biology and Chemistry and an Ordinary-level Mathematics credit. The previously reviewed Information Science IB Diploma policy remains the only copy in the IB catalogue.
- [Nairobi's Architecture department](https://architecture.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-architecturebachelor-architectural-studies) lists three Advanced GCE principal passes with a separate English requirement. The [faculty page](https://built-environment.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-architecturebachelor-architectural-studies) lists two. The dataset records both and keeps BAS-to-BArch progression separate. The [Nairobi Quantity Surveying](https://recmqs.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-quantity-surveying), [Actuarial Science](https://mathematics.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-science-actuarial-science) and [Data Science](https://mathematics.uonbi.ac.ke/admission-content-type/bachelor-science-data-science) pages give course-specific KACE and KCSE conditions.
- [Chuka Actuarial Science](https://www.chuka.ac.ke/course/bachelor-of-science-in-actuarial-science/) lists KCSE, KACE and diploma routes for self-sponsored applicants. JKUAT distinguishes its [Quantity Surveying](https://www.jkuat.ac.ke/bachelor-of-quantity-surveying-4-years/) mature-entry policy and [Actuarial Science](https://www.jkuat.ac.ke/bachelor-of-science-in-actuarial-science/) diploma advanced standing from first-year entry.
- [USIU-Africa](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/international-applicants/) lists IB Diploma consideration and separate credit awards for individual IB results. The SAT or ACT submission rule applies to US citizens and permanent residents. The institution's [English proficiency requirements](https://www.usiu.ac.ke/admission-requirements/) give the TOEFL and IELTS requirements. The reviewed current materials supply no AP admission cut-off.
