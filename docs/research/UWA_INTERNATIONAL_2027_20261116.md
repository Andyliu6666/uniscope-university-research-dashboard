# University of Western Australia international school-leaver entry, 2027

The [2027 International Course Guide](https://www.assethub.uwa.edu.au/api/public/content/6c9d60476b3d4e75afe43f5efb9b9373?v=0781fb37) gives the undergraduate programme ranks on p. 116 and qualification equivalences on p. 117. Specific award and pathway rows add selection and English requirements. The archived PDF SHA-256 is `19fe5fcf3e8c7b1ba2b3d4527fd540a8e2ab53e6e4ff56f910efed5d15f54128`.

- The seven published rank columns give complete IB Diploma totals, UK/Cambridge GCE aggregates, Singapore-Cambridge aggregates and US Advanced Placement aggregates. The [international equivalents page](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/pathways-and-eligibility/international-and-overseas-qualifications) explains the AP grade threshold, certified exams and the recognised secondary qualification. The [IB page](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/pathways-and-eligibility/international-baccalaureate) provides the English and mathematics equivalents.
- Common rank and scoring rules are reused across degrees. The data retains other country qualifications including Gaokao, HKDSE, CBSE, CISCE, KCSE, STPM, UEC, GSAT and South African NSC. English requirements in the programme tables are kept alongside the institutional English policy. Specific rows retain selected audition, subject, English and clearance requirements.
- The Medicine, Dentistry and Podiatric Medicine entries describe the international school-leaver pathways, with the subsequent university progression conditions kept in the route text. Entry remains competitive. Medicine and Dentistry use the ISAT and interview under the [2026/2027 international health guide](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/-/media/documents/admissions-guides/2026-2027-international-health-admissions-guide_web.pdf). The [Podiatric Medicine course page](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/courses/doctor-of-podiatric-medicine) describes its academic ranking and selection GPA condition.
- The published Law course row gives IB 37 for the standard Juris Doctor school-leaver pathway and IB 40 via the Bachelor of Philosophy (Honours). UWA's equivalence page gives the 96-rank GCE conversion but lacks a corresponding AP figure. The standard pathway leaves that AP value unassigned.
- Music/Science has the same IB total alongside ATAR 70 and 75 in different guide sections. The detailed [Assured Pathways](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/explore-courses/assured-pathways) page agrees with the course-specific architecture and public health degree rows at ATAR 90 and IB 32, while the guide overview shows ATAR 92. Some teaching rows differ between the dedicated international guide and the general assured pathways page. The data records why programme and intake scope matter in these cases.

The University publishes separate clinical guidance for 2028 and separate graduate-entry eligibility. This batch covers only the named 2027 school-leaver routes.
