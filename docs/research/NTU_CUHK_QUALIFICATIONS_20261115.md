# NTU and CUHK undergraduate qualifications

## Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

NTU's AY2025–26 Indicative Grade Profile shows grades of applicants offered places, including offers under aptitude-based admissions. PDF pages 2–3 give the 10th and 90th percentiles of three H2 grades and one H1 grade for 43 named options and three groups of double-major options. The calculation assumes grade C for General Paper and Project Work. Pages 4–5 give 35 Singapore polytechnic GPA bands. The 11 rows whose sample was too small retain the suppression note. Each grade profile represents historical offers; check the current year for subject and eligibility criteria. [Indicative Grade Profile](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/undergraduate-admissions/igp/ntu_igp.pdf)

The 2027 IB matrix gives minimum subject requirements for the 43 matched named options. The records preserve alternatives, level distinctions, and relevant assessment instructions. The application guidance supplies the separate Medicine UCAT and reference conditions. The 2027 subject matrix gives no numerical IB total for these programmes. Options outside the Indicative Grade Profile matches remain to be reviewed. [2027 IB subject matrix, pages 2–8](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/undergraduate-admissions/msr/emsr_ib.pdf) · [NTU general admissions](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admissions/general-admission-requirements)

The American high school diploma route uses five AP exams with scores of 4 or 5, with a three-exam application exception for Singapore citizens and permanent residents. The evidence records the scope of AP Calculus BC and, where Physics is required, Physics C. The international matrix gives separate conditions for applicants using AP as a supplement to other qualifications. The UK A-level route specifies four passes in one sitting and publishes exceptions that allow three. The UK A-level supplement route has a separate three-subject rule. Each route stays separate from the Singapore-Cambridge cohort and its historical grades. [American high school diploma](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admissions/general-admission-requirements/international-qualifications/american-high-school-diploma) · [UK A-level requirements](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admissions/general-admission-requirements/international-qualifications/uk-a-level-certificates-cambridge-pre-u) · [International subject matrix, pages 9–10](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/undergraduate-admissions/msr/emsr_intnl.pdf)

## Chinese University of Hong Kong

The 2027 IB sheet lists subject preferences for first-year IB Diploma applicants. The Local Non-JUPAS and non-local downloads were byte-identical on November 15. Forty-three distinct choices are represented. Forty-two reuse the previous batch's programme entries, and Robotics and Smart Engineering adds one entry with the 2027 brochure's confirmation caveat. The Learning Design and Technology record preserves the different faculty wordings. The sheet specifies separate language provisions with a grade of 4 for the stated English and Chinese cases. The 2026 historical table remains attached to its original intake. [2027 IB preferences, pages 1–4](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/IB_subject_preference_2027entry.pdf) · [Non-local copy](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/adm/intl/adm_guide/IB.pdf) · [Robotics and Smart Engineering](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/programme/rosen/) · [2027 undergraduate brochure, page 6](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/CUHK-NJL-2027.pdf)

CUHK's international admissions reference gives the middle 50% of historical admissions. IB includes core points. The published ranges are 36–42/45 in 2023 and 37–43/45 in 2024 and 2025. The three years each give 144–168 UCAS Tariff points for three A-level subjects. These records represent university-wide historical results. [Historical admissions score reference](https://cuhk.edu.hk/adm/intl/adm_guide/Admissions_Score_Reference.pdf)
