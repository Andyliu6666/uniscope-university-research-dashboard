# Lesotho programme links and admission pathways

The 2026/2027 catalogue review covers eight Agriculture awards and the Bachelor of Education in Special Education. It links the reviewed shared rules for Agriculture, Education and Health to their named awards. The source for those groups is the prospectus copy downloaded November 8, 2026, SHA-256 `2ebaf68e39593508490f79f4b22981201672d727b2875ba0c9aac6810526f123`. The retained pages and later website sources were reviewed November 12. A fresh Word download failed at DNS resolution. [Official 2026/2027 prospectus](https://nul.ls/download/prospectus-2026-2027-final-version/)

- Printed pages 8–11 list eight Agriculture awards under indirect entry. The Year 1 direct section names Agriculture and Agricultural Economics. The five Agriculture specializations share the diploma-level Mathematics C condition for the specified diplomas. The undated faculty page gives one additional C and two D grades, while the cycle-specific direct entry section gives two additional C and one D. The new note records that difference and the published scope. [Agriculture faculty programme page](https://nul.ls/faculty-of-agriculture/academic-programmes/)
- Printed pages 12–14 set the BEd and Special Education grade and indirect-entry conditions. Special Education also requires an introductory course during the first year for students intending to major in that area. The undated course page names Mathematics C for Business Education, while the cycle-specific Special Education subsection names the English Language Education specialization. The applicable Business Education rule remains unresolved. [Special Education course page](https://nul.ls/course/bachelor-of-special-education/)
- Printed pages 16–19 support the shared ranking association for Nursing and Midwifery, Pharmacy, Environmental Health and Nutrition. The shared subject-grade rule on pages 18–19 is specific to Environmental Health and Nutrition. [Official 2026/2027 prospectus](https://nul.ls/download/prospectus-2026-2027-final-version/)
- Social Work's course page swaps the Year 2 and Year 3 order printed on prospectus page 44. Faculty regulations FSS 3.05–3.06 allow consideration for first-year semester exemptions for credit holders of both diploma types. Placement remains unresolved. [Social Work course page](https://nul.ls/course/15077-2/) · [Faculty regulations](https://nul.ls/faculty-of-social-sciences/faculty-regulations/)
- The faculty describes a Mathematics bridging route at grade C for six named programmes and grade D for Urban and Regional Planning. Both require the remaining entry requirements and availability after the first admission round. The page gives no intake year, so the bridging scopes display an unspecified year. [Social Sciences programme page](https://nul.ls/faculty-of-social-sciences/academic-programmes/)

The review found no published numerical IB minimum for these routes. NUL's similarly named 2026/2027 application-requirements download is linked under postgraduate. The undergraduate link still points to the older-named checklist. The Physical Education and Sport entry requirements are listed separately and the download remains to be reviewed.
