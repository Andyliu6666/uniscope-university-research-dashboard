# UWA international health admissions for 2028

- The [2028 International Health Admissions Guide](https://www.assethub.uwa.edu.au/api/public/content/5b39d57ad21941fdb9cfd14682d80a72?v=e73a9840) is listed on the university's [course guides page](https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/explore-courses/our-courses-explained/course-guides). The reviewed PDF has SHA-256 `d0adda102fe688aaef1f2a0f0289afe178f3279c6376a82edc48ad7fca20e087`. Its course tables run from pp. 13–19, graduate entry from pp. 21–23 and the assured-pathway timeline is on p. 30. Tuition headings refer to 2027 prices. The data in this batch covers admissions and excludes those prices.
- The two source artifacts contain 22 international school-leaver IB totals, using the same programme identities as the 2027 guide. They include the published GCE aggregate and example grades, Singapore A levels, CBSE, HKDSE and Canadian figures. The 2028 Nursing row gives British Columbia and Ontario 70, and the Social Work row gives 60. Medical Science prints GCE 14 and ABB, and Social Work prints GCE 7 and CCD. Both figures are retained for these two routes because the examples and aggregates diverge under the standard GCE scale. The health guide does not provide AP figures, so the previously verified 2027 AP scores remain in their dated scopes.
- The school-leaver route to Medicine and Dentistry uses IB 40 and GCE 15 (AAA) for competitive consideration. ISAT meets the 25th percentile in both sections. Final ranking uses school rank or equivalent, interview and ISAT at 30%, 50% and 20% respectively. Podiatric Medicine uses IB 35 and GCE 14 (AAB), with no ISAT or interview. The application schedule gives 1 January through 30 June 2027 for the 2028 intake. The programme rows and the timeline have distinct locators.
- Fifteen graduate degrees have separate selection requirements. Medicine and Dentistry use the recognised bachelor's degree and Selection GPA 5.5 floor, with GAMSAT or MCAT. Dentistry also provides Canadian and US DAT alternatives, with their respective sections and scales. Other routes cover prerequisite university study, weighted average marks, IELTS, course-specific checks and quotas where published. Each graduate programme has a separate identity from the school-leaver pathway to the same degree.
