# University of Auckland New Zealand school-leaver entry, 2027

This batch attaches domestic school-leaver admissions to the same 35 programme records used for the overseas secondary route. Cambridge qualifications taken in New Zealand use the local tariff rank. Each IB row comes from the programme rank table. Published guaranteed ranks require University Entrance and all applicable admissions conditions.

- [Updated 2027 undergraduate prospectus](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/assets/study/programmes-and-courses/undergraduate-study-options/undergraduate-prospectus/UGP_2027_web_final%202.pdf), printed pages 90–93. Its Business and Property rows now give NCEA 200, Cambridge tariff 200 and IB 27. The [prospectus index](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/undergraduate-study-options/undergraduate-prospectus.html) confirms that the newer Cambridge and NCEA requirements supersede the original printing. The revised PDF SHA-256 is `02d9d56ae3321152c996048bee91cb11f0d6cd48eaa8917e72abc0a481dd206e`.
- [Health study update](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/assets/fmhs/study-with-us/docs/health-study-changes-2027.pdf), printed page 17. The restructured Biomedical Science, Health Sciences and six listed BSc health majors use NCEA 200, Cambridge 200 and IB 28, with recommended preparation and general university eligibility. These are the faculty's entry thresholds. Nursing has separate requirements. The PDF SHA-256 is `b69c6bfce77c33c9acc72387b08ffb326289699983e502602c688b10119ec49c`.
- Engineering domestic Cambridge uses tariff 300 for the single degree and 320 for the conjoint, with Mathematics and Physics at full A Level. The corresponding IB diploma ranks are 32 and 34 with both subjects at HL. The overseas grades are published in their separate programme records.
- Architecture, Dance, Teaching, TESOL and Music retain the relevant portfolio, interview, test, safety or audition conditions. Component rules and the greater rank apply to conjoint degrees. The general catalogue has no standalone numeric LLB Part I cutoff, and the Law progression records cover eligibility through the other degree and Part II selection.

AP and overseas high-school alternative scores are covered by the separate overseas admissions batch. The 2028 school-leaver rank table has its own admission year.
