# University of Mauritius Science and Medicine and Health Sciences, 2026/2027

The university's two faculty tables cover six Science codes and four Medicine and Health Sciences codes. Mathematics and Physics options sharing one code retain the original code in their sources. Twelve Science entries and four health entries are separately searchable.

- [Science faculty requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gRmeUcHk-DBK1qflQBY7hJhbIfHwBR4u/view), PDF pages 3–4. SC320 and SCE321 require A-level Mathematics at C or above. SC345 Physics options require A-level Mathematics and Physics passes. The source also specifies different science subject lists and O-level credits for Marine Environmental Sciences, Biology and Chemistry. The [faculty offering list](https://www.uom.ac.mu/FOs/index.php/programmes/undergraduate) identifies the individual Mathematics and Physics titles.
- [Medicine and Health Sciences requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ShnYfsXcPWd1Y8sHd0Ujr0jj648pOFAC/view), PDF pages 3–4. MH331 specifies three science A levels at CCC and five O-level passes including Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and English. It lists possible interviews for international applicants and a hepatitis B immunisation requirement. Nutritional Sciences has subject-specific foundation modules. Occupational Therapy lists three science passes. MH438 includes the initial Diploma or degree route, a qualification-based upgrade and separate experience-based consideration.
- The faculty sheets refer to approved equivalents. Their academic figures describe GCE/HSC entry, with no numeric IB or AP threshold in the reviewed sheets. The programme links reuse the general recognition and eligibility rules from the university's 2026/2027 regulations.
