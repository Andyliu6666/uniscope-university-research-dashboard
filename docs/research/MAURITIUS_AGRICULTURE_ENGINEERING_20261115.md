# University of Mauritius Agriculture and Engineering entry, 2026/2027

The [official faculty index](https://www.uom.ac.mu/index.php/individual-programme-entry-requirements) publishes the 2026/2027 routes by faculty. This initial batch covers all six Agriculture and ten Engineering codes, including a part-time planning diploma and its separate top-up degree.

- [Faculty of Agriculture requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AwTyMks4Rfi7uCBP_nqMgeDTX9uBXJey/view). Codes A312, A341 and A342 share approved science passes and first-year foundation conditions. A343 and A405 use the same Mathematics and Chemistry O-level credits and related A-level eligibility. A400 has a different O-level science condition. SHA-256 `de2f725fd100e3db361a0c8cbbfaa7b02be0a3af8970dc74fa68c92c1691b6a9`.
- [Faculty of Engineering requirements](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jZlZMJADXzVhn2GNsQIDD2Yb2Xl9Iqm6/view). The batch records the named Chemical, Civil, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Electrical and Telecommunications requirements, as well as Design, Supply Chain and Town Planning. E404 requires O-level Chemistry and Physics credits and A-level passes in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. SHA-256 `a926fdd8e2c6cd304d2e6ef4649fe4dfca530f78613f41b35f6d1d5bfb9b14f8`.
- [General admission regulations, Chapter 1](https://www.uom.ac.mu/images/FILES/Regulations/2026_2027/chap1.pdf) publish the IGCSE plus IB route without an IB total and describe the HSC/GCE degree criteria. [International admission regulations, Chapter 14](https://www.uom.ac.mu/images/FILES/Regulations/2026_2027/chap14.pdf) give separate HSC or London GCE arrangements, application evidence, TOEFL/IELTS equivalence and possible SAT use. They supply no AP or numeric test threshold for these general alternatives.
- The Engineering sheet refers E201 to the general degree requirements, while the academic regulations contain a diploma-specific section and Chapter 14 provides a separate international diploma condition. The profile records these source distinctions. E412 requires prior Diploma study or a department-approved equivalent.

The university publishes these faculty PDFs through links on its official index. The two exact file links were verified before import.
