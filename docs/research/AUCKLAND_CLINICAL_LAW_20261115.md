# University of Auckland clinical and Law progression, 2027–2028

The five degrees have separate post-secondary selection routes. The clinical records cover progression after the first qualifying university year and the published international graduate or tertiary-study routes. Medicine, Optometry, Pharmacy and Medical Imaging have no direct school-leaver scores for the clinical stage in the reviewed sources.

- [Medicine](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/bachelor-of-medicine-and-bachelor-of-surgery-mbchb.html) requires a first-year interview eligibility GPA of 6.0 in both cycles. The 2027 route assesses seven courses for interview eligibility and weights four core course grades, UCAT ANZ and the MMI for the final selection. The 2028 route uses four core courses and a minimum B across the eight courses, with Casper and the MMI each contributing half of final selection. The international graduate route has separate criteria.
- [Optometry](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/bachelor-of-optometry-boptom.html?intEntryReq=yes) uses GPA 5.5 for interview consideration. [Pharmacy](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/bachelor-of-pharmacy-bpharm.html) uses GPA 5.0 for first-year progression and 4.0 for the international tertiary route. The 2027 Pharmacy page includes Otago in its domestic section, while its international first-year section describes the Auckland degrees. The 2028 sections include qualifying Otago Health Sciences First Year applicants.
- [Medical Imaging](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/Medicalimaging.html) puts the four-course 2028 GPA floor at 5.0. The [central enrolment guidance](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/applications-and-admissions/enrolment/choosing-your-courses/first-year-enrolment-for-undergraduate-health-programmes.html) lists 5.5. The data presents both values with their provenance; confirmation is needed. Both sources require grades of at least B in all eight courses.
- The [2027 health study transition notes](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/assets/fmhs/study-with-us/docs/health-study-changes-2027.pdf) describe the expanded first-year programmes and the 1 July 2027 deadline for 2028 clinical entry. The common Auckland courses and deadline are stored once and linked to the four degrees under 2028 progression.
- [Law](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/bachelor-of-laws-llb.html) has distinct Part I and Part II scopes and a separate graduate aptitude route. The dedicated [Part II policy](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/law/current-students/llb-information/apply-llb-ii.html) changes LAW 121G from a passing grade in 2027 to C+ or higher in 2028. Each year's LAW 131 and LAW 141 floor is C+. An eligible LawGPA of 6.50 provides a guaranteed place under the published policy.
