# Hong Kong international qualification scores

## University of Hong Kong

The November 2026 brochure for 2027 entry gives 59 numeric programme rows on PDF pages 2–4. The international and Non-JUPAS scheme includes local and non-local applicants with international qualifications. The batch preserves each published programme grouping as one application choice and records competitive references for IB, A-level and AP. The Social Sciences Elite row retains the approval condition. University-wide eligibility is recorded once. [2027 brochure](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2026-11/HKU%20Intl%20Admissions_2027.pdf) · [Admissions scheme](https://admissions.hku.hk/apply/international-qualifications)

AP rows retain the number of exams at each grade and the SAT condition or AP-only alternative. HKUMed confirms its 2027/28 Medicine reference of six AP exams at grade 5 together with SAT 1500 or ACT 34 in one sitting. It publishes a separate 2028/29 reference of at least eight AP exams at grade 5 with the same SAT or ACT condition. The faculty page also supplies the Distinguished MedScholar track's 45 IB points at the first attempt and five A* grades at the first attempt in one exam series. Other DMS qualifications are assessed individually. Biology or Chemistry, a working knowledge of Chinese and the optional 2027/28 UCAT are recorded separately for the main Medicine route. [HKUMed Non-JUPAS and DMS requirements](https://hkumed-ugadmissions.hku.hk/ug_programmes/bachelor-of-medicine-and-bachelor-of-surgery/)

## Chinese University of Hong Kong

The Local Non-JUPAS page links the reference table under 2026 entry. The five-page PDF is internally dated October 2025 and uses admissions data from 2023–2025. All 85 rows were checked. The 14 repeat appearances of joint programmes had identical IB, A-level, SAT and AP values and yield 71 distinct application choices. Each citation lists the applicable PDF pages. The historical AP ranges and legacy SAT Subject Test column remain historical references. [Local Non-JUPAS downloads](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/application/non-jupas/important-information/) · [2026 programme reference scores](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Reference_Scores.pdf)

Both first-year routes publish general eligibility of IB 30/45, three A-level passes or two A-level plus two AS-level passes, and two AP exams at grade 3 with a senior secondary diploma plus SAT 1190 or ACT 24. These are university-wide eligibility criteria. The batch records this policy once with the year unspecified. [Local requirements](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/application/non-jupas/general-requirements/) · [Non-local requirements](https://admission.cuhk.edu.hk/application/overseas-other-qualifications-non-local-international-team/requirements/)
