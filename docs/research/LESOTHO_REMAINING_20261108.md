## Interpretation and deduplication

Humanities relevant-diploma entry lists credit/Second Class and pass plus two years' work without an OR/AND connector. It remains unknown rather than imposing work universally or waiving it. COSC class wording is read with the existing general rule. Diploma APS32 and COSC aggregate described as 40 or better are not bachelor's APS26, IB totals or independently invented grade conversions. International CHE evaluation for higher-education/transfer applicants remains distinct from ECOL school conversion.

Six Science continuation options share completed BSc General Year1; Biotechnology expressly requires Biology/Chemistry Year2. Different major combinations do not multiply catalogue entries. Computer Systems and Networks, Electronics Engineering and BSc Electronics retain their actual diploma fields and the narrower/wider printed equivalence wording. Environmental Science is not Environmental Health. Department review does not become a guaranteed entry year. English D versus general C remains qualified; Computer Science counts as science only in the explicitly named direct-entry programmes. Human Geography's two-other-C/one-D pattern differs from Planning's one-other-C/two-D pattern.

Commerce's IEMS distinction OR CAS qualification alternatives are shared once. Marketing/HRM's additional external-qualification clause is not copied to Accounting. Economics's credit route specifically requires both Quantitative Techniques and Business Statistics C. The Advanced Diploma in Social Work appears in two bullets assigning Year3 and Year2; neither year is chosen silently. Demography's pass-plus-work bullet specifies two years after graduating, and its connector to credit entry remains unclear. Public Administration/Political Science double-major options retain one award identity each.

ODL is recorded as distance because the source calls it ODL, with the stated mixed delivery explained; it does not promise fully online study. Adult Education's Third Division COSC clause conflicts with the general First/Second Class wording and is not treated as an established waiver. Adult Education and Management diplomas require six LGCSE subjects but state grades for only five; the missing threshold is not borrowed from Mass Communication. Mass Communication requires English and Sesotho or an accepted alternative language. RPL forms, evidence and assessment stay in their actual indirect routes; no work duration or test pass mark is invented. No additional numerical IB/AP requirement was found in these reviewed chapters.

## Shared-reference implementation

Optional programme `sharedRequirementKeys` references must each resolve to one fact in a university-wide profile of the same exact institution and study level. Ambiguous/missing/duplicate and programme-specific references are rejected. The importer resolves catalogue IDs first, then saves their applicability in the one fact's provenance metadata. API output resolves current names within the parent institution/level; the page displays an “Applies to” list. Year, intake, delivery, applicant and entry route remain those of the parent profile. Associations are explicit, not automatic inheritance of every general rule.

No new database table, dependency or migration is introduced. Omitted optional fields do not alter completed artifact serialization. Existing fact/programme rows remain unchanged; changing a stored association on a skipped fact requires review instead of an overwrite. The quality gate checks empty/malformed/duplicate/orphan and cross-institution/level links, using safe UUID validation and indexed programme lookup. Source-backed programme identities and reviewed group scope remain separate from the absent IB records for most catalogue awards.
