# University of Cape Town Commerce, Health Sciences and Law

Reviewed November 15, 2026. These 28 additional entries draw on the [2027 UCT undergraduate prospectus](https://mig-commerce.uct.ac.za/sites/default/files/media/documents/2027-uct-undergraduate-prospectus-1-april-2026.pdf) and the [2027 application directions](https://mig-commerce.uct.ac.za/sites/default/files/media/documents/ug-directions-for-applicants-2027.pdf).

- Commerce pages 29–30 show 18 standard plans and four Actuarial Science and Quantitative Finance award-and-plan combinations. The table lists local scores of 36 or 40. Records retain Cambridge qualification combinations, subject grades, the six-subject IB Diploma rule, the distinction between Mathematics HL 5 or SL 6 and Mathematics HL 7, and NBT literacy thresholds. The prospectus uses subject points and programme eligibility when evaluating international qualifications.
- Health Sciences pages 41 and 45–47 give separate country eligibility lists and documented exceptions for MBChB, Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology. MBChB shows a 40-point minimum and 46-point likely-offer reference on the UCT scale, together with Proficient NBT. The four rehabilitation degrees show references of 36 and 44 and Higher Intermediate NBT. Subject rows distinguish Cambridge A-level and AS-level requirements and IB HL and SL grades. [Faculty admissions overview](https://health.uct.ac.za/home/undergraduates/prospective-students/admissions/undergraduate-eligibility-and-process).
- Page 62 gives 44 and 46 local APS points for international applicants to the four-year undergraduate LLB, with a limit of ten international admissions. The directions specify Academic Literacy for this applicant group. The prospectus describes Academic and Quantitative Literacy in the broader LLB selection process. Both locators remain in the data.

The degree-specific IB records explain the UCT admissions conversion and its programme context. These values are reported on UCT's admissions scale. The existing United States AP exemption record remains available on the institution profile.
