The [UMS admissions overview](https://www.ums.edu.my/v6/index.php/academic/admission-ums) and its [separate international-admissions portal](https://smp.ums.edu.my/admission) distinguish the application routes. The final review corrected both UMS records from `all` to `applicantType: "domestic"`, conservatively representing the published UPU/local route. Their applicant context and conditions explicitly say that applicability to international admissions has not been verified. This is not a claim that every historical UPU cycle has had identical citizenship rules. UniKL's expressly labelled local requirement is also stored as `domestic`. These three local-route scores must not appear as verified international-applicant requirements.

Every entry year is `unspecified`. A current review date, website footer, Sunway's 2026 fee estimate, and Sukkur's curriculum dates are not evidence of an IB admission year. Named intake months are retained where the page supplies them. These are published academic minimums and carry no admission guarantee.

Only UMS publishes the programme codes used here. Other course codes remain null; UniKL's MQA/MOHE accreditation identifiers and degree abbreviations were not substituted for course codes. Core-point inclusion remains null except for Sunway's express exclusion.
