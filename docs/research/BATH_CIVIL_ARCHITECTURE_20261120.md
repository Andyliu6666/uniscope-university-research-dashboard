# Bath 2027 Civil Engineering and Architecture admissions

- The [Civil Engineering directory](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/civil-engineering/) lists eight first-year BEng/MEng routes. The [Architecture directory](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/architecture/) lists three first-year BSc/MArch routes, alongside a separate two-year MArch entry route that requires separate review. The eleven reviewed routes have distinct UCAS codes.
- [Civil Engineering](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/civil-engineering/beng-civil-engineering/) and the Structural and Architectural Engineering variants publish IB 36 with HL 7/6/6, including either HL Mathematics course. The contextual offer keeps 36 and uses HL 6/6/5 with Mathematics at 6. Standard Level Mathematics is excluded. A-level offers are A*AA or contextual AAB with Mathematics at A. The AP route needs three grades of 5 including Calculus BC, with Calculus AB excluded.
- [Architecture](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/architecture/bsc-architecture/) publishes IB 36 with HL 7/6/6 and at least 6 in Mathematics or Physics. Contextual offers keep the total and use HL 6/6/5. The source also publishes exceptional SL routes. Its AP route accepts Calculus AB, Calculus BC or any Physics AP within the three grade-5 results, with at most one Calculus exam. It requires GCSE Mathematics and English at 6/B and Category A English equivalents. The engineering courses use Category C English and GCSE English at 4/C.
- Architecture normally assesses the UCAS application and does not consider portfolios. Its vocational and Access routes have additional art/design preparation and interview conditions.
