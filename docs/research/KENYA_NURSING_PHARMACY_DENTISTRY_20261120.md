- The [KUCCPS 2025/26 cut-off archive](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_CUTOFFS_14-07-2025.pdf) supplies 46 offered course codes across the Nursing, Pharmacy and Dental Surgery groups. Forty-five reconcile to 35 existing directory institutions. Uzima's Nursing course remains pending an institution match.
- The [national 2025 cluster guide](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_CLUSTER_DOCUMENT_2025_03.pdf), p. 7, assigns Pharmacy and the listed Nursing qualifications to cluster 13B. Its subject minimum is C+ in Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics Alternative A or Physics, and English or Kiswahili. Dental Surgery shares cluster 13A with Medicine and requires B in those four subject groups. These requirements apply within the historical domestic central-placement scope.
