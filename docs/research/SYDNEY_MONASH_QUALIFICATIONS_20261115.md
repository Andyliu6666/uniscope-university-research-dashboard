# University of Sydney and Monash University undergraduate qualifications

Reviewed November 15, 2026. First-year international entry for 2027.

## University of Sydney

The [2027 international admission guide](https://www.sydney.edu.au/dam/corporate/documents/study/how-to-apply/international-admission-guide.pdf) supplies 89 distinct course titles on PDF pages 1–4. Each source row was checked against the rendered table, its CRICOS code and its qualification columns. Shared registration codes are recorded in the course context so that individually titled rows remain selectable.

- All 89 course rows have an indicative IB total. The GCE entries distinguish the three-subject and four-subject columns. ACT and SAT figures retain their respective route and scale. The guide also gives course-level English test scores and a common validity rule.
- Science/Medicine and Arts/Medicine each show 45/45 for IB and 24 points for four A2 subjects. The three-subject GCE column is marked inapplicable. The corresponding official course pages document the school-leaver selection and additional assessment. [Science and Medicine](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/courses/courses/uc/bachelor-of-science-and-doctor-of-medicine.html), [Arts and Medicine](https://www.sydney.edu.au/courses/courses/uc/bachelor-of-arts-and-doctor-of-medicine.html).
- The course table has no AP cut-off column. The shared AP record covers the [recognised US qualification route](https://www.sydney.edu.au/study/applying/how-to-apply/undergraduate/recognised-qualifications.html) and the guide's ACT/SAT supporting-results provision. Programme scope notes retain printed selection markers and the [2027 Veterinary Science commitment statement](https://www.sydney.edu.au/content/dam/courses/science/2027-bvb-dvm-commitment-statement-form.pdf).

## Monash University, Australian campuses

The [2027 international course guide](https://www.monash.edu/__data/assets/pdf_file/0005/3941744/undergraduate-international-course-guide.pdf) and individual course pages support 20 additional entries across Art, Design and Architecture, Arts and Humanities, Education, Law and Pharmacy. The batch adds 19 numeric IB, GCE and AP sets, with AP scores on the two-exam scale. Music and Education retain their additional selection criteria. [Music auditions](https://www.monash.edu/arts/music-performance/audition-information), [Teacher education assessment](https://www.monash.edu/education/future-students/teacher-education/situational-judgement-test).

- The Architectural Design and Architecture F6003 course page gives IB 26/45, GCE 8, AP 6/10 and SAT 1160/1600. The combined-degree row in the guide leaves these cells blank. [F6003 international entry](https://www.monash.edu/study/courses/find-a-course/architectural-design-and-architecture-f6003?international=true).
- Fine Art retains diploma completion and portfolio selection. Its course page does not give a fixed IB total. [F2003 international entry](https://www.monash.edu/study/courses/find-a-course/fine-art-f2003?international=true).
