# Bath 2027 Pharmacy and Pharmacology admissions

- [Pharmacology](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacology/) publishes three routes (M110, M111, M112); [Pharmacy](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacy/) publishes three (M100, M102, M103). All are Bath awards. M102 and M103 are delivered at the University of Plymouth.
- [Pharmacology](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacology/bsc-pharmacology/) lists a typical IB 36 with HL 6/6/5. [Direct Pharmacy](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacy/mpharm-pharmacy/) lists 35 with the same HL pattern. Both require Chemistry and a second qualifying subject, and both publish separate contextual and exceptional Standard Level rules. Across five direct routes, the pages give AAB at A level and the AP pattern 5/5/4, with Chemistry and a second science or mathematics subject in both qualification systems.
- [M103](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacy/mpharm-pharmacy-with-preparatory-year-at-the-university-of-plymouth/) publishes three Home IB offers (28, 32 and 35) and three A-level offers (CCC, BCC and ABB). The combination of science and mathematics subjects determines the applicable offer. The programme requires Home eligibility and offers separate vocational and progression rules. There is no AP threshold in the course page's US qualifications section.
- [M100](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacy/mpharm-pharmacy/#application-information) gives 12 January 2027 for Overseas applicants and 13 January 2027 for Home applicants. [M102](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/pharmacy/mpharm-pharmacy-at-the-university-of-plymouth/#application-information) gives 13 January for both. M103 lists 13 January for Home applicants. Selection interviews and fitness-to-practise rules apply to Pharmacy, while Pharmacology usually assesses the UCAS application with specified interview exceptions.
