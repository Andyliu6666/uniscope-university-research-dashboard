# Kenya Computing and Medicine admission requirements

- The [July 2025 KUCCPS cut-off report](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_CUTOFFS_14-07-2025.pdf) and [2025/26 programme table](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_PROGRAMMES_2025.pdf) join on exact programme codes. The earlier table's 2022 and 2023 values agree with the later report for every numeric cell reviewed in this batch.
- The 47 matched Computer Science courses require KCSE Mathematics Alternative A and Physics at C+ and English or Kiswahili at C in the dated 2025/26 source. Twelve matched Medicine courses require Biology and Chemistry at B, Mathematics Alternative A or Physics at B, and English or Kiswahili at B. These central-placement rules share the cycle's general C+ mean-grade requirement, with distinct course subject thresholds.
- The datasets contain 137 admissions facts, 352 published historical score cells and 175 course-to-rule links across 59 courses at 48 institutions. Thirty-eight links reuse existing rules. Each score retains its KCSE examination year and the publication cycle. Figures from this archive are historical selection outcomes.
- Institution identity matches use directory names and reviewed aliases. [Mama Ngina confirms its relationship to Kenyatta](https://audit.mnu.ac.ke/about-us/), [The East African University identifies itself as TEAU](https://teau.ac.ke/), and [Tom Mboya describes its former university-college identity](https://tmu.ac.ke/history/history.php). The Garissa, Taita Taveta and Uzima directory matches remain pending. No new institution identity was inferred for them.
- Programme-specific subject cells from pp. 4, 5 and 8 were extracted and their layout reviewed. The 78-page table's SHA-256 is `b7e783b9854e933344f3c4edd1a832d137d7c3a5be55564aa88db01b84298552`. Computer Science and Medicine are separate artifacts so each fits the existing import format. Medicine depends on two mean-grade links introduced by the Computing artifact and was previewed after Computing was imported.
