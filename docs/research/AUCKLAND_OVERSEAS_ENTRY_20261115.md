# University of Auckland overseas entry, 2027

This batch covers first-year undergraduate entry with secondary qualifications earned outside New Zealand. An exact programme code and the 2027 catalogue selection were checked before processing each table. Twenty-nine tables contain 600 qualification rows, including country-specific GaoKao, school diploma and examination routes. Six BSc majors have additional grades in the table notes. The source does not specify the intake in each score row. The URL's Summer value selects a catalogue view.

- [Arts overseas entry](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BA) illustrates the programme rows and common qualification notes. Three full A levels are counted, with General Studies excluded and academic H2 subjects used in Singapore. The US AP score aggregates two to four certified exams with at least 3 in each. General Educational Development (GED) has a separate baseline and transcript conditions apply to the US diploma route.
- [Engineering (Honours)](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BE%28Hons%29) lists IB 32 with Mathematics and Physics at HL, A-level BBB with Mathematics and Physics and AP aggregate 12 with Calculus and Physics. The [Arts/Engineering conjoint](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BA%2FBE%28Hons%29) lists IB 34, A-level ABB and AP aggregate 12 with the respective subject requirements.
- [Science](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BSc) gives separate note rows for Cell and Molecular Bioscience, Exercise Sciences, Medicinal Chemistry, Nutrition, Pharmacology and Physiology. These six majors share IB 28, A-level BBC and AP aggregate 9. The Malaysia UEC note for those majors marks the result to be confirmed.
- [Music](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BMus) publishes IB 26 and A-level CCC in the country table while its programme description says the figures await confirmation. The IB row is a competitive reference and the general facts explain the source discrepancy and creative selection.
- [Nursing](https://study.auckland.ac.nz/ords/r/uoa/catalogue/program?p0_catalogue_term=Summer&p0_catalogue_year=2027&p5_code=BNurs) also requires a satisfactory interview. The A-level overview specifies English and a science subject. Some USA rows still mention SAT Subject Tests, so the relevant fact asks applicants to confirm current options.
- Some catalogue tables list Indonesia SMA/MA scores while their programme overviews describe a foundation or tertiary qualification route for Indonesian school-leavers. Both signals are retained where they occur. Vietnam's two unfilled programme grade cells retain the explicit grade condition from the note.
- The [health study transition](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/fmhs/study-with-us/study-options/undergraduate-study-fmhs/health-study-changes.html) identifies distinct 2027 and 2028 clinical progression rules. Medicine, Medical Imaging, Optometry and Pharmacy are left to an intake-specific pathway batch. The main overseas Science, Health Sciences and Biomedical Science totals reflect limited or provisional selection in their catalogue descriptions.
