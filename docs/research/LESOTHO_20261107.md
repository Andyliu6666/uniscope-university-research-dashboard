## Scope and qualification decisions

- LGCSE APS uses six recognized subjects, with A*=1 through G=8 and a ceiling of 26: lower is better. COSC has its separate First/Second Class and aggregate-34 condition. Neither is an IB total.
- General English C versus Agriculture/Science Education D remains unresolved. General foreign-qualification ECOL evaluation versus the printed GCSE exemption remains qualified. The two fee-category systems remain unresolved rather than choosing one silently.
- Agriculture's diploma credit/Year 2 and diploma-level Mathematics C are kept distinct. The STC bullet's relationship to the diploma requirement lacks an explicit OR/AND connector. Shared named award groups remain in university-wide scope; their individual catalogue entries are not claimed complete.
- BEd and Special Education share school/English-teaching conditions and teacher-placement rules. BEd Business/Economics has its additional Mathematics C. The BSc with Education route has Mathematics B and science-specific teacher preparation. STC starts in Year 1; the secondary diploma starts in Year 2. Part-time Primary Education is not classified as distance merely because it is part-time. Special Education has shared named requirements but no separate catalogue declaration in this increment.
- Nursing and Midwifery requires school-science B; Pharmacy requires school-mathematics B. Environmental Health and Nutrition share their C-grade school baseline. Their merit-ranking formula is stored once. Nursing's indirect paragraph groups IB/A-level/degree qualifications with alphabetic D grades without explaining the numerical IB mapping; no conversion is invented.
- Pharmacy's diploma-credit OR diploma-pass-plus-two-years-work alternatives remain separate from A-level and IB. The latter is recorded only in the structured IB artifact: **HL Biology 4, HL Chemistry 4, and HL Mathematics OR Physics 3**, without an overall total. It belongs to indirect entry (`other`), not the five-year school-leaver route. The IB year field uses entry year 2026; the general artifact retains cycle 2026/2027, with the full cycle named in both records.
- Environmental Health explicitly prints diploma OR BSc. Nutrition lists a credit diploma and BSc without a connector; eligibility on either alone or both together is not assumed. ODL Nursing is a distinct diploma-based degree: nursing diploma with credit, Council registration, and Midwifery only for the two named intended majors. Its explicit delivery mode is distance.
- LLB has three routes within one award: direct five-year, other-discipline degree with Second Class Second-Division for three-year, and BA Law pass for two-year. The direct preamble's stray “3-Year” text is recorded as a source issue. English Literature/History/Development Studies are tie-breakers, not invented universal prerequisites.
- The Insurance certificate is recorded in other-level shared scope, not a false bachelor's entry. Its two-year insurance-work-evidence bullet has unclear applicability to school versus post-secondary entrants. A possible blended approach is not turned into a guaranteed mode.
