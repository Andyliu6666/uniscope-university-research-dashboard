# Singapore admissions qualifications and degree options

## Nanyang Technological University

The November 2026 edition of the 2027 IB matrix extends the earlier 43 selected titles. This pass covers 75 additional decision rows on PDF pages 2–8, including second majors, double degrees, dual subject choices, and combined science programmes. When a block has identical subject prerequisites, the data keep the named options together. A different subject level receives a separate row. For example, Maritime Studies with Business or Sustainability requires SL Mathematics, and the Data Analytics option requires HL Mathematics. The Physics options retain which choices the school marks for selective assessment. The matrix lists alternative degree formats in footnotes, which do not supply separate subject conditions.

The admissions facts include the Communication Studies selection test/interview and the mother-tongue requirement for citizens and permanent residents. Subject-only records keep the whole-diploma total empty. This matches NTU's explanation that the IB sample does not support a meaningful grade profile. [2027 IB matrix](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/undergraduate-admissions/msr/emsr_ib.pdf) · [IB admissions and mother-tongue instructions](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admissions/general-admission-requirements)

## National University of Singapore

Tables 1–3 give AY2025/26 historical offer profiles and places actually taken up across 21 application choices. The grade bands are the 10th and 90th percentiles of offers, including Aptitude-Based Admissions. The A-level format uses three H2 subjects and one H1 subject. NUS assumes a C in General Paper and Project Work for that historic calculation. Seventeen programme choices have a published polytechnic GPA pair. Four GPA pairs are suppressed because of small samples. Group figures remain on the named combined choice and exclude double degrees. NUS gives a small sample as its reason for withholding its historical IB score profile. [AY2025/26 indicative grades and intake](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/indicative-grade-profile)

The December 2025 single-degree tables give 23 distinct named options for 2026. Their IB and Singapore-Cambridge A-level records keep different subject levels, qualitative grade wording, alternatives, and application ranking rules. The faculty brochure confirms Law's IB English score of 5 and its SAT 700 plus IB English score of 4 alternative. NUS publishes these as language conditions within the programme, and neither route establishes a total out of 45. For Geospatial Intelligence, the programme page confirms CHS entry requirements and gives no additional high-school subject threshold. [IB single-degree table](https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/docs/default-source/default-document-library/ibdp-sp.pdf) · [Singapore-Cambridge A-level subject table](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/docs/default-source/admissions/h1-h2-sdp.pdf) · [NUS Law 2026 brochure, printed page 26](https://law.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-NUS-Law-Undergraduate.pdf) · [Geospatial Intelligence](https://chs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/gi/)

The 2026/27 international standardised-test guide sets grade 3 per AP exam. Its three combinations are five AP exams, three AP exams and SAT, or three AP exams and ACT with Writing. It specifies the minimum SAT and ACT components, accepted AP subjects, score reporting and the Calculus BC requirement with the Law exception. The international A-level qualification page asks for four good passes and names rules for candidates applying with pending results. The international prospectus separately gives three as the baseline with good grades in four preferred. The website retains both statements with their respective sources. [Standardised-test guide, pages 1–3](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/docs/default-source/default-document-library/standardised_test.pdf?sfvrsn=7cbaf22d_8) · [International A-level qualification table](https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/international-qualifications-for-foreigners/international-qualifications/international-a-level) · [International prospectus, printed pages 10–11](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/docs/default-source/nus-publications/intlprospectus.pdf)

NUS's 2027 IB route publishes the application window, transcript requirements, and additional Dentistry and Medicine requirements for May 2027 predicted results. Dentistry requires AP plus SAT or ACT with Writing. Medicine allows UCAT UK/ANZ or AP plus either of those tests. The data keep this 2027 route separate from the 2026 subject matrix and standardised-test guide.
