# Nottingham Malaysia 2027 undergraduate admission requirements — 20 November 2026

This batch covers the 36 undergraduate-entry course pages linked by the [official undergraduate index](https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Undergraduate-courses/Find-an-undergraduate-course/Find-a-course.aspx). Each page explicitly identifies **2027 entry**. These are the Malaysian campus requirements, using existing ROR `04mz9mt17`; UK and China campus offers are not substituted. Integrated MEng and MPharm courses remain undergraduate-entry routes. Intake term is unspecified because no individual intake is assigned by the qualification tables.

## Data and scope

Two general-admissions artifacts contain **151 distinct facts** with **453 course-to-rule links**; 35 links in part 2 reuse rules reviewed in part 1. The matching IB artifact contains **36 programme-specific numerical minima**. There are 36 new catalogue identities. The first general artifact contains 81 facts across first-year and transfer scopes; the second contains 70. They must be imported in part order before the IB artifact so that cross-part references and course identities resolve.

The general facts include A-level, STPM, UEC, Australian Year 12, Ontario OSSD, Gaokao plus Chinese high-school diploma, Indian CBSE/CISCE, AP, local diploma, external foundation, Nottingham foundation, English evidence and additional course-specific conditions. Qualification routes are alternatives. A-level exclusions, subject grades, school-level prerequisites, waiver conditions, case-by-case consideration and qualification duration are retained. School-wide ownership is used to store a shared condition once; its course associations define applicability.

## Boundaries and source differences

- Computing requires IB HL Mathematics AA or AI at 5; Mathematics and Data Science explicitly requires AA HL at 5. Its AP Calculus **BC** 4 condition is narrower than Computing's AP Calculus 4. No unstated Calculus variant is assigned to engineering.
- Engineering's HL 5/4 combinations keep the published subject set without assigning a specific grade to one subject where the source does not. Nutrition's Mathematics/Physics alternative expressly excludes taking both for its two-science condition. Biomedical Science and Pharmaceutical and Health Sciences have distinct Chemistry/Biology conditions.
- BPharm has IB 30 with HL 5/5/5 and explicitly rejects AP as an entry qualification. MPharm has IB 32 with HL 6/6/5 and AP 5/5/4. These are separate course identities. Neither rule is a universal pharmacy minimum.
- Local diploma consideration for Year 2 is scoped as `transfer`. Both Pharmacy pages expressly prohibit Year-2 local-diploma entry while retaining a separate Year-1 GPA 3.50 route. The common denial is stored once. Foundation/Matriculation/Diploma applicants' five school-level B grades remain conditional on that route.
- English policies retain IELTS/PTE scores, legacy TOEFL thresholds for tests through January 2026 and the separate scale for tests from February 2026, component minima, validity periods, accepted school qualifications, OSSD full-credit/fast-track conditions, medium-of-instruction letters, test-location and possible MOHE documents. Different published grades or documentary conditions are kept separate, including inconsistent GCSE/IGCSE wording; no silent reconciliation is made. Spacing, punctuation, introductory phrases and a stray repeated “Grade” token alone do not create an additional policy.
- The no-prior-language-knowledge condition belongs only to the French/Spanish International Relations minor routes. It is not assigned to the plain International Relations degree. A pharmacist's post-study provisional registration condition is outside this admissions batch.
- These current course pages supersede neither all historical requirements nor unrelated applicant routes. Older 2020/2021 prospectuses and the general 2026 brochure were not used to fill 2027 qualification cells. No unpublished competition, essay, interview or score was inferred.
