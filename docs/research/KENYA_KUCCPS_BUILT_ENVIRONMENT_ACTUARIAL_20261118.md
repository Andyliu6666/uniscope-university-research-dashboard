# Kenya KUCCPS placement requirements

- The [2025/26 cut-off report](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_CUTOFFS_14-07-2025.pdf) has 44 pages, 2,122 distinct programme codes and 71 provider labels. Each numeric column is a previous KCSE examination-year cut-off. The archived file's SHA-256 is `ce43a4e94a9e0f56e28c1b0814c5cab1e831b29aab3799e8181b111ad78bf6e5`.
- The first-page batch covers Architecture, Quantity Surveying and Actuarial Science. Thirty-eight entries reconciled to 31 directory institutions. Garissa University's programme 1096107 is pending an institution identity. [Tom Mboya University confirms its former University College identity](https://tmu.ac.ke/history/history.php), which is the label of active ROR `03gzm8w29`.
- The [2025 cluster sheet](https://statics.kuccps.net/uploads/globalFiles/DEGREE_CLUSTER_DOCUMENT_2025_03.pdf) identifies the field requirements on pp. 5 and 7. The built-environment course routes require KCSE Mathematics Alternative A, Physics and English or Kiswahili each at C+ or above. Actuarial Science requires Mathematics Alternative A at C+. The cycle notices for [2025/26](https://kuccps.net/degree-application-20252026-placement-cycle) and [2026/27](https://ftp.kuccps.net/index.php/kuccps-opens-application-window-2026-placement-universities-and-colleges) provide the general C+ mean-grade requirement for degree placement.
- The 2026/27 course and institution listings were checked for 16 programme codes. Their 2025 KCSE figures and later backfills have a separate cycle scope. For example, [the Architecture listing](https://students.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1004/) shows Nairobi 42.737 for 2025, and [the Quantity Surveying listing](https://students.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1005/) shows Nairobi 38.756 for 2025. The [Actuarial Science listing](https://students.kuccps.net/programmes/detail/1010/) supplies later 2024 figures for Kisii and Tharaka where the PDF had a dash. A dash remains missing data. Older values repeated by the portal stay in their archival scope.
- [KUCCPS defines cut-offs](https://www.kuccps.net/frequently-asked-questions) as the weighted cluster score of the last candidate placed competitively. Programme records retain the underlying KCSE exam years. These scopes cover domestic first-year central placement. Direct-entry foreign qualifications require separate university sources.
