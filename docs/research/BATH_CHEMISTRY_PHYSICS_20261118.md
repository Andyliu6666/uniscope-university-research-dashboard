# Bath 2027 Chemistry and Physics admissions

- The [Chemistry directory](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/chemistry/) and [Physics directory](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/physics/) identify 12 and 19 routes respectively. The course pages have 31 distinct UCAS codes. Placement and integrated degree variants remain tied to their own course pages.
- [Chemistry](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/chemistry/bsc-chemistry/) has a typical IB offer of 36 with Chemistry at HL 6 and a qualifying HL subject at 5 or above. The contextual total is 35 and the subject conditions remain. The A-level pattern changes between AAB and AAA with the number of additional science or mathematics subjects. The AP offer is 5/5/4, including Chemistry at 5 and another science or mathematics subject at 4 or higher, plus the published mathematics requirement. The separate sources for drug discovery and management match these criteria.
- [Physics](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/physics/bsc-physics/) publishes IB 36, A-level A*AA with Mathematics and Physics, and AP grades of 5 in Calculus BC, Physics 1 and Physics 2. The reviewed variants in Astrophysics and Theoretical Physics use the same thresholds. The [joint Mathematics and Physics degree](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/physics/bsc-mathematics-and-physics/) requires HL Mathematics Analysis and Approaches for IB and Further Mathematics for the standard A-level route. All four joint course pages describe the AP policy as under review.
- [N114](https://www.bath.ac.uk/courses/undergraduate-2027/physics/mphys-physics-with-professional-placement-research-placement-or-study-abroad/) independently lists a September 2027 start, an Overseas closing date of 13 January 2027 and a Home field dated January 2028. Its scope includes the discrepancy and confirmation guidance. The other 30 routes list 13 January 2027 in both fields.
